Common Questions

Got Questions?

When is my mooring location revealed?+
We send full location details, GPS coordinates, parking instructions, and local recommendations exactly 48 hours before your check-in time. The anticipation is part of the experience — but rest assured, every mooring is carefully chosen.
Can I request a specific mooring?+
We work hard to surprise and delight, but if you have a specific mooring in mind (or wish to avoid one) just let us know via WhatsApp after booking and we'll do our best to accommodate.
Is the boat suitable for dogs?+
Absolutely — dogs of all sizes are very welcome. All of our moorings have great nearby walks, and we keep the boat clean for your furry travel companions. No extra charge.
What are the check-in and check-out times?+
Standard check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out by 10:00 AM. We're often flexible on these — just ask when booking and we'll see what we can do. Late checkout is sometimes available for a small fee.
Do I need boating experience?+
None whatsoever — the boat stays moored throughout your stay. You enjoy all the atmosphere of canal life without any need to steer or operate the boat. Just arrive, relax, and enjoy.
How do I pay?+
After confirming via WhatsApp we'll send a secure payment link. We accept all major cards. A 25% deposit secures the booking with the remainder due 14 days before arrival.
