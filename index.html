01 / Swarkestone

Lock-side evenings with a village glow.

Swarkestone brings the stay into classic Derbyshire: low towpaths, warm pub windows, and easy lock-adjacent arrival. It is the stop for a first glass by the water before the canal settles into night.

Best for guests who want the romance of a quiet village with practical arrival close by.
Crewe and Harpur The Brookfield Garden centre cafe Close parking
02 / Weston on Trent

A slower village pause before the reveal deepens.

Weston on Trent has a softer rhythm: good food, a nearby spa, and a tucked-away waterside feel that makes the mystery mooring concept feel genuinely private.

A low-key luxury stop for long lunches, spa time, and a calm return to the boat.
Coopers Arms Bengal Kitchen Secret Garden Spa Very close parking
03 / Aston Lock

Heritage paths, village essentials, and fireside food.

Aston Lock feels quietly lived-in, with pubs, village shopping, and walkable arrival options. It is the kind of stop where the luxury is not spectacle, but ease.

For guests who like their escape grounded in local texture and a proper village walk.
The White Hart The Malt Co-op nearby Walkable parking
04 / Shardlow

The canal village with old-world theatre.

Shardlow is the cinematic heart of the corridor: historic brick, inn lights, and the feeling that the waterway has been keeping secrets for centuries.

The strongest heritage mood, ideal for guests drawn to atmospheric pubs and canal history.
The Navigation Inn The Malt Shovel Clock Warehouse Village store
05 / Sawley Lock

Broad water, generous choice, and an easy evening out.

Sawley Lock gives the escape a more sociable edge, with restaurants, pubs, and supermarkets within reach while the boat remains held in its own quiet world.

A polished practical stop for guests who want choice without losing the canal-side hush.
Harrington Arms Esquina Tapas White Lion Major supermarkets
06 / Trent Lock

Where the waterways meet and the night opens out.

Trent Lock has a junction-side drama: water movement, pub terraces, and the sense of arriving somewhere hidden in plain sight.

For guests who want the most cinematic waterside ending to the route.
Steamboat Inn Trent Lock Pub Golf & country club Lock Lane access
